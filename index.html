VFH uploader

VFH uploader is a private tool for the YouTube channel Vanished & Forgotten History. The channel's owner uses it to upload the channel's own videos through the YouTube Data API: it sets their titles, descriptions and publish times, adds captions, and checks the result.

It is not offered to the public. It acts only on the owner's channel, and only after the owner signs in with Google and grants access.
