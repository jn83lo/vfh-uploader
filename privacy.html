VFH uploader: privacy policy

Effective 1 November 2026.

What it accesses

With the channel owner's permission (Google scope youtube.force-ssl), VFH uploader can manage videos, captions and playlists on the signed-in YouTube channel. It uses this only to upload the owner's own videos, set their details and schedule, add captions and playlists, and read them back to confirm they are correct.

What it stores

One Google sign-in token, kept as an encrypted secret in the owner's private repository. It does not collect or store information about viewers or anyone else.

Sharing

No data is sold or shared. VFH uploader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube

VFH uploader uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at Google Account → Third-party apps & services.

Contact

Through the Vanished & Forgotten History YouTube channel.
